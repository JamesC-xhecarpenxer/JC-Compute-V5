# KCR Optimization

KCR optimization is a policy problem over semantic equivalence classes.

## Objective

Maximize query-preserving compression while controlling representational variance.

## Metrics

- `KCR`
- `H_rep`
- `dQ`

## Interpretation

- increase `KCR` when semantic preservation holds
- reduce `H_rep` when deterministic representatives are desired
- shape `dQ` when representative geometry matters
